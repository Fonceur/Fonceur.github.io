Android

Get the app

Free, with no ads and no account.

Features

  • Real-time prices for over 2,300 Québec gas stations (regular, premium, diesel), with 30-day history and regional averages
  • Over 19,000 EV chargers from Circuit électrique and Open Charge Map, filtered by level and connector
  • Multi-stop trip planner with suggestions along the way (cheap gas, chargers, rest areas, attractions, parks); on Android, hand the trip off to Google Maps or Waze
  • Québec's official scenic routes, STQ ferry schedules, highway rest areas, over 4,800 tourist attractions and 49 parks
  • Price alerts and a home-screen price widget
  • CarPlay and Android Auto: nearby stations and your active trip on the car screen
  • Light or dark mode, 5 languages (French, English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese), VoiceOver and TalkBack support

What's new

  • iPhone and iPad release notes, every version
  • Android release notes, every version

Data sources

  • Régie de l'énergie du Québec: gas prices
  • Le Circuit électrique and Open Charge Map: EV chargers
  • OpenStreetMap: map and station amenities
  • Société des traversiers du Québec: ferry schedules
  • Transports et Mobilité durable Québec: highway rest areas
  • Tourisme Québec / SIT Québec: tourist attractions
  • Tourisme Québec / Données Québec: scenic routes
  • Ministère des Ressources naturelles et des Forêts: parks; Wikipedia: park descriptions
  • OpenRouteService: trip routing
  • Statistics Canada: average retail gas prices
