Tromi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Patrick Roy ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tromi mobile application for Android, iPhone and iPad ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices. It covers both versions of the App; where they differ, the difference is noted.

Information We Collect

Location Data

The App requests access to your device's location (precise/GPS or approximate; on iPhone and iPad, only while you are using the App) to:

  • Show gas stations and EV charging stations near you on the map
  • Calculate distances to stations
  • Sort stations by proximity
  • Display nearby stations on Android Auto or CarPlay
  • Start a new trip in the trip planner from where you are

Location data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. It leaves your device only through the trip planner, as described under Network Requests below: a new trip starts from your current position, whose coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to fetch a readable place name, and the coordinates of your trip stops are sent to OpenRouteService when you tap "Compute trip" (or "Recompute"). If you deny location permission, the App falls back to a default postal code you can configure in Settings.

No personal information or device identifiers are sent to these services, and your location is sent only as trip-stop coordinates to Nominatim and OpenRouteService, as described here. Geographic bounding boxes used in API queries cover broad areas and cannot identify individual users. Trip-planner requests to OpenRouteService include the coordinates of the stops you assembled — these are only sent when you tap "Compute trip" or "Recompute", are not associated with any account or identifier, and are not stored beyond what OpenRouteService logs to operate the service. Requests to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) are made only when a trip stop is placed from a location — when you drop or GPS-locate a stop, and automatically for a new trip's starting stop when location is enabled (the stop's coordinates are sent to fetch a readable label) — or when you use the trip planner's "Choose a contact" option (the chosen contact's postal address is sent to convert it to coordinates). They carry no account or identifier. The User-Agent sent to Wikipedia, Nominatim and Overpass (and, on iPhone and iPad, to OpenRouteService) identifies the app and its current version (e.g. "Tromi/1.x") plus a public Google Play Store URL — it does not include any user data.

To keep cached data, price alerts, and the home-screen widget current, the App also repeats these public-data requests periodically in the background (by default about every 12 hours, adjustable in Settings) using Android's WorkManager or, on iPhone and iPad, iOS Background App Refresh, which iOS schedules at its own discretion and which you can turn off in the iOS Settings app. Background refreshes fetch only the public datasets for the layers you have enabled — they send no location.

Contacts

The trip planner's optional "Choose a contact" feature ("From a contact" on iPhone and iPad) uses the system's built-in, permission-free contact picker, on Android as on iPhone and iPad: the system shows your contact list, and only the single postal address you tap is handed back to the App. The App never requests the contacts permission and cannot read your contact list — it only receives the one address you choose, which it then forward-geocodes via Nominatim (see above) to place a trip stop. The address is not stored beyond the trip stop's resulting coordinates and label.

External Links and Navigation Apps

Some screens open external web pages in a browser tab (a Chrome Custom Tab on Android, a Safari view inside the App on iPhone and iPad) — for example the Québec 511 road-conditions map, EV incentive program pages, Statistics Canada, park, attraction and scenic-route web pages, and the data-source credits. Those pages are loaded by your browser and governed by the respective site's own privacy policy; the App passes them nothing beyond the link itself. When you choose to navigate to a station, park, or trip stop, the App hands that destination's coordinates and name to the navigation app you use (e.g. Google Maps or Waze on Android, Apple Maps on iPhone and iPad), which applies its own privacy policy.

Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored on your device only, in the App's database and settings (Room and DataStore on Android; SQLite and UserDefaults, in a container shared with the App's widget, on iPhone and iPad):

  • Favorites (gas stations, EV chargers, ferry routes, rest areas, tourist attractions, parks)
  • App settings and preferences (display mode, language, search radius, default postal code / location)
  • Cached station, charger, ferry, rest-area, attraction, and park data
  • Cached park boundary polygons fetched on demand
  • Cached park entry points (welcome/visitor-center locations from OpenStreetMap)
  • Cached Wikipedia park descriptions (article snippet + URL)
  • Price history snapshots (30 days)
  • Saved multi-stop trip plans (stops + computed road polyline + per-leg distance/duration, plus any non-routed places you keep on the trip)
  • Price alert configurations
  • Cached Statistics Canada gas-price chart data
  • The last OpenRouteService quota reading (the shared daily request budget shown in the trip planner)
  • The station chosen for each home-screen price widget

This data is never transmitted off your device by the App, apart from the trip-stop coordinates sent for routing and place names as described under Network Requests.

On iPhone and iPad, this data is included in your device backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer), like other apps' data, so restoring a backup brings back your favorites, trips and price alerts. Those backups are handled by Apple and by your computer under your own account, not by us. On Android, the App excludes this data from device backups.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry services
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties

Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications for price alerts you configure. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no push notification service is involved.

Android Auto and CarPlay

The App declares the androidx.car.app.MAP_TEMPLATES permission so it can display nearby stations and your active trip on an Android Auto screen. This permission grants access to car-screen templates only — it does not collect any data from you or your vehicle.

On iPhone, the App uses Apple's CarPlay fueling and EV charging capabilities to display nearby stations and chargers, your favorites, and your active trip on a CarPlay screen. CarPlay shows the App's screens on the car display only — the App does not collect any data from you or your vehicle.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the App collects no personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes all stored data; on iPhone and iPad, a copy may remain in earlier device backups until they are deleted or replaced. Price history snapshots are automatically pruned after 30 days.
