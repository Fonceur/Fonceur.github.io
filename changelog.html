Release notes for every version of Romi, newest first. Back to the Romi page

2.23 October 2026

  • New Wild card choice: the special jokers of Rummikub with a Twist (Double, Color-change, Mirror)
  • New setting, Frozen jokers: a joker on the board can only be taken back with the card it stands for
  • New opening options: No minimum and Wait a turn after opening
  • Multiplayer lobbies show the host's Wild card setting
  • The full version of Romi now plays big tables of 2 to 8 players

2.22 September 2026

  • Color, Symbol and Background now work on every tile preset — your first edit starts a custom palette, and asks before replacing the one you saved
  • Multiplayer lobbies: unavailable buttons now say what's missing (your name, the PIN, more players)
  • With the system's high-contrast text setting, buttons that can't be used are now hidden instead of looking active

2.21 September 2026

  • New: take back a wild card from the board by dragging onto it the card it stands for — screen readers get a matching action
  • Fixed the computer adding a card to a set instead of taking back the wild card
  • Fixed the card tiles going black under the system's high-contrast text setting
  • Fixed the Undo and Redo buttons being invisible in dark mode

2.20 September 2026

  • Fixed a start-up crash after an update and the dark theme inverting on the latest Android
  • New screen reader support for the board and your rack, with actions to play or take back a card
  • Fixed online games losing cards at the deal and opponent card counts that kept growing
  • Online end-of-game table: each player's record for that table size, correct numbers after a draw, solo statistics no longer altered
  • Nearby games are now shared between more versions of Romi

2.19 September 2026

  • New tile face colour and number font, with a Night preset for dark tiles
  • New Photo background: use one of your own pictures as the table background
  • New vibration feedback, even when you play with the sound off
  • Online tables of 2 to 4 players are now shared between Romi, Romi Lite and Romi Maxi
  • Fixed jokers going astray when tapped, and made online games steadier

2.18 September 2026

  • Customize tiles: colors and symbols for each suit, plus presets
  • New layout options: hide buttons, choose the panel side, bigger cards
  • AI players can fill empty seats in multiplayer games
  • New welcome screen, "What's new" dialog and a Romi vs Romi demo
  • Fixes: card lost when undoing a draw, wrongly revoked opening, end-of-game winner, multiplayer freeze when the pile ran out, placement and steals
