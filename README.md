# Fonceur.github.io

GitHub Pages user site for the Romi and Tromi apps, served at https://fonceur.github.io/.

- `index.html` / `index-fr.html` — landing page linked from the Google Play and Amazon Appstore listings (store links, rules in 18 languages, release-notes link, a footer link to the Tromi page). English by default; a French browser is redirected to the French page unless a toggle choice is stored in `localStorage`.
- `images/` — launcher icon and a downscaled board screenshot (`docs/images/anglais/Screenshot_20251227_210141.png` in the Romi repo), plus `og.jpg`, the link-preview image the Open Graph tags of the Romi landing, changelog and privacy pages point at (not `doc/`, a verbatim mirror) (the Play feature graphic `docs/images/Android.png`, 1024×500, converted to JPEG at quality 85 — 72 KB instead of 400 KB, under the ~300 KB WhatsApp drops; the tags declare that size, so keep them in step if the graphic changes).
- `app-ads.txt` — authorized sellers for AdMob and Amazon Publisher Services; both crawlers fetch it at the domain root.
- `romi-privacy.html` / `romi-privacy-fr.html` — the Romi privacy policy (all editions, one document), linked from every store listing.
- `doc/` — the in-app rules documentation, a verbatim copy of `romi/src/main/assets/doc*.html` and its images from the Romi repo. Refreshed by `tools/publier-site.sh` in the Romi repo (run by its `ship` skill); do not edit here.
- `changelog.html` / `changelog-fr.html` — the Romi release notes, every version since 1.85 newest first, maintained by `tools/publier-notes-version.py` in the Romi repo (run by its `/changelog` command and `ship` skill) from the English and French Google Play blocks of its `docs/CHANGELOG_*.md`. **These pages are the archive:** the script reads back the versions already here and only adds or replaces those it has a changelog file for, so versions whose changelog was pruned from the Romi repo (1.85-2.19) live only here and in git history. Do not edit here, except to drop a version: delete its `<section>` from both pages. A version with no `v<version>` tag yet is labelled "Upcoming update" until the script is re-run after tagging.

- `tromi/index.html` / `tromi/index-fr.html` — the Tromi landing page, served at https://fonceur.github.io/tromi/ (App Store and Google Play badges, screenshots, features, release-notes links, data sources). It is also the Marketing URL of the App Store listing. Same language redirect as the Romi page; both read the same `localStorage` key, so a toggle choice on one carries over to the other.
  - **Platform switch** (iPhone · Android · Both): it shows one platform's screenshots, store badge and release-notes link, or both. The first visit picks from the device: an iPhone or iPad gets iPhone (an iPad's Safari presents itself as a Mac, so a Mac with touch counts as an iPad), an Android device gets Android, anything else Both. A choice made on the switch is kept in `localStorage` under `platform`, the same on both languages. The page marks the `<html>` element with `data-platform` before it draws, and the CSS hides the other platform's `.ios` / `.android` elements. Without JavaScript there's no switch and both platforms show.
- `tromi/changelog.html` / `tromi/changelog-fr.html` — the Tromi for Android release notes, every version newest first, maintained by `tools/publish_changelog_page.py` in the Tromi repo (run by its `/changelog` skill) from the English and French sections of its `docs/CHANGELOG_*.md`. **These pages are the archive:** the script reads back the versions already here and only adds or replaces those it has a changelog file for, so a version whose changelog is pruned from the Tromi repo stays here. Do not edit here, except to drop a version: delete its `<section>` from both pages. A version with no `v<version>` tag yet is labelled "Upcoming update" until the script is re-run after tagging.
- `tromi/changelog-ios.html` / `tromi/changelog-ios-fr.html` — the Tromi for iPhone and iPad release notes, the same archive rules, maintained by `scripts/publish_changelog_page.py` in the Tromi-iOS repo (run by its `/changelog` command) from the English and French sections of its `docs/CHANGELOG_*.md`. A version is dated once its `v<version>` tag (on the commit of the build Apple approved) exists. Do not edit here.
- `tromi/privacy.html` / `tromi/privacy-fr.html` — the Tromi privacy policy in English and French, generated by `tools/publish_privacy_page.py` in the Tromi repo from its `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY_FR.md` (run by its `/sync-docs` skill after any policy edit; it refuses to publish when the translation no longer mirrors the English). Do not edit here: the next run overwrites them.
- `tromi/images/` — the launcher icon (`docs/images/tromi_icon_512.png` in the Tromi repo, downscaled to 192 px); three Android screenshots per language, `{en,fr}-*.jpg` (`docs/images/phone/{en,fr}/01_map.png`, `02_station_detail.png`, `05_trip_map.png` in the Tromi repo, downscaled to 480 px wide JPEG); the same three for iPhone, `ios-{en,fr}-*.jpg` (`docs/images/iphone/{en,fr}/` in the Tromi-iOS repo, the 6.9" App Store set, downscaled to 480 px wide JPEG at quality 80 with `sips`); the official store badges, `badge-app-store-{en,fr}.svg` (Apple's black badge, `toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/{en-us,fr-ca}`, as downloaded) and `badge-google-play-{en,fr}.png` (`play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/{en,fr}_badge_web_generic.png`, cropped to the badge itself: Google's PNGs carry transparent margins, different in each language, and the Play badge must show at least as large as the App Store one; the page sets both 48 px tall). Don't recolour or redraw the badges, and keep the trademark lines in the footer that both stores' badge rules ask for. The folder also holds `og-en.png` / `og-fr.png`, the link-preview image every Tromi page's Open Graph tags point at (the Play feature graphics `docs/images/tromi_play_store_{en,fr}.png`, 1024×500, copied as-is — the tags declare that size, so keep them in step if the graphic changes).

The older copy of the Tromi privacy policy, the separate [tromi-legal](https://github.com/Fonceur/tromi-legal) project site (https://fonceur.github.io/tromi-legal/, synced by hand), is being retired in favour of `tromi/privacy.html`. Keep it up until the Play Console and App Store Connect privacy URLs point at the new page, then turn it into a redirect rather than deleting it, so older links keep working.
